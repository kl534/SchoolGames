# Cat sound sources

All are real cat recordings from Wikimedia Commons, released under CC0 (public domain, no
attribution needed; credited here anyway). Each was trimmed, high-passed at 150 Hz, loudness
normalised and saved as 64 kbps mono mp3. `sfx.meow`, `sfx.yowl` and `sfx.hiss` in
`src/shared/audio.ts` play them.

- `meow-1.mp3`, `meow-2.mp3`, `meow-3.mp3`: the 1st, 2nd and 3rd meows of
  [Black cat sound begging the foods.flac](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Black_cat_sound_begging_the_foods.flac)
  (by 매일 누군가가 도둑맞을 때마다), raised 4 semitones (same length) so they sound like a kitten.
- `yowl.mp3`: the 1st meow of the same recording at its own pitch.
- `hiss.mp3`:
  [Cat hissing - Zabuhailo.wav](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cat_hissing_-_Zabuhailo.wav)
  (by Zabuhailo, via Freesound), 0.95 s to 2.05 s.
